# Why D1 / D2 look worse than the old sheet, and how to get the sheet's look back (research, 2026-10-20)

Answer to `prompts/2026-10-20-research-mud-look.md`, from `results/2026-10-20-mud-two-designs-ds.md` (one DS run) and the
code at 3ccdf35. Research only. Labels: PROVEN-CODE (file:line), MEASURED (DS logs), [repo], INFERENCE.

## 0. Short answer
My two designs each removed one of the two things the sheet's look is made of:
- **D1 removed the soft layer.** Its surface is clamped to the felt rut floor (MUD_MudOverlay.c header line 7:
  `h = max(undug + (d - THICK), dug)`), so a press can never go deeper than the voxel rut: 0 cm on pass 1, 3 cm per
  later pass. The sheet's depth came from pressing into its own 15 cm layer from the first pass.
- **D2 removed the simulation.** It lowers a lifted layer only where the felt floor is cut or worn - no stamps, no
  ridges, no slump, on the 0.25 m patch grid, at the patch mesh pace.
The sheet's look = a raised soft layer AND its own stamp + slump simulation at 0.15 m, updated near the wheels at 10 Hz.
Neither part alone gives it. D1 was also expensive because of the extras I added around the sheet maths (re-centre,
ground reads per rebuild, replay), not because of the maths itself.

Recommended route: **un-park the old MudSheet itself in a local, decoupled mode** - its own look code (which you
already liked), client-only, no sync layer, none of its hooks into the rut code, tiles only where wheels went, fading
to the ground at their edges. Add it to the numpad 3 switcher next to D2 so one DS session compares them.

## 1. Why D1 and D2 look worse (Q1)

## 2. Which sheet look parts matter, and their cost (Q2)
Ranked by how much of the look they carry (INFERENCE from the user's words "how it deformed and flowy it was" and from
what D1 / D2 lacked) with the cost measured on the sheet where we have it:
| rank | part | what the eye gets | cost (sheet, DS client) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | own soft depth from pass 1 (raised 0.15 m layer, GRADUAL press) | the tyre visibly sinks at once; deep grooves | needs a raised layer (tiles) - see sec 3 |
| 2 | ridges + bow wave from the pushed volume | mud pushed up beside / ahead of the tyre | inside Stamp ('sh wheels' 0.1-0.46 ms/frame incl. drag) |
| 3 | slump | rounded, settling, "flowy" shapes | local 10 Hz Slump over the live window - not measured on a DS (once moved from every frame to 10 Hz: "~3 ms of ~5" per frame before, MUD_MudSheet.c ~846 comment); est. 0.3-0.5 ms/frame |
| 4 | live 10 Hz rebuild under the wheel | the rut follows the tyre instantly | 'sh upload' 0.4-0.84 ms/frame |
| 5 | texture drag (+ LimitDrag) | smeared mud | inside Stamp |
| 6 | SMOOTH_EDGE + taper + skirts | no blocky quads, no gaps | inside the tile build (~0.88 ms per build) |
| 7 | 0.15 m grid (vs 0.25) | tyre-width detail | ~2.8x the vertices of the patch grid per m2 |

Option A details (all switches exist in MUD_MudSheet / MUD_LookRuts / MUD_VoxelProbe; PROVEN-CODE for the gates):
- `ENABLED` true, `SYNC` false -> no op tag (MUD_VoxelProbe 3396 needs ENABLED && SYNC), no RUT_SHEET_MS pacing
  (RutUnderSheet needs SYNC, 1428-1434), no MUD_MudSync work (OnHere needs SYNC), local stamps + local 10 Hz Slump
  (MUD_MudSheet ~844-852: `!SYNC` path).
- `ONE_GRID` false (TakeOp / CoversCell need ONE_GRID, 2748-2806), `LAZY_RUT_MESH` false (else patch meshes are
  hidden under the sheet), `MUD_LookRuts.SHEET_LOOK` false AND `SHEET_SKIP` false (else look ruts are redirected or
  skipped under the sheet, MUD_LookRuts 239, 368, 512, 609), A2 patch lift stays off (PatchLiftOn needs SYNC).
  These are the only places the sheet changes rut behaviour; with them off the voxel ruts run exactly as now.
- Where it draws: tiles only where this client stamped (SPARSE idea, but WITHOUT the A2 patch lift): a built tile's
  open side fades to the ground over EDGE (the fade must treat "no sheet tile" as open - today HasTile = covered
  ground, deep-dive option 1). Untouched ground: no tile, no lift, no grass change, no prone issue there.
- Known gaps to fix in the same build: one live window follows the nearest vehicle only (FOLLOW), so a second truck
  elsewhere leaves no mud - stamp other vehicles into stored tiles (data) within view range; crater / dig rebuilds in
  the blast frame and DigNow taper neighbours (research/2026-10-19-whole-map-sheet-issues.md sec 2-3).
- Still open: grass on the raised band (the old zone sheet had the same; the user liked it, so check by eye).

## 4. Keep the switcher
The numpad 3 cycle with the DbgUI label (MUD_MudOverlay.c:157-213) is the most useful thing this round produced: one
launch, every look side by side. Keep it as the permanent mud-look A/B tool and add mode "sheet local" to it, so the
cycle is off -> sheet local -> D2 presets -> D1 (until D1 is archived) -> off.

## 5. Smallest DS test (one session, one build)
Build: option A behind its own mode in the numpad 3 cycle (defaults off). DS + PeerPlugin1, robot reverse circle
(2 min) on grass, >= 6 runs per mode for the numbers.
1. off: baseline MUD ms / fps while driving (the 00:25 'off' rows were standing - not a baseline).
2. sheet local: MUD ms, 'sh wheels' / 'sh upload' / 'sh stored' / slump ms; by eye: does it deform and flow like the
   old sheet; tile edges fade into the ground (no step); grass on the band; the felt rut still felt.
3. D2 preset 4: the same numbers, for the cost / look comparison.
4. Crater + short trench in a sheet area: holes clean, no late tiles.
5. A second vehicle away from the first (does it leave mud?) and a client that joins late (what it sees).
Kill A if: MUD script while driving > ~3 ms/frame over 'off', or it does not look like the old sheet.
