# B1 - Dual Contouring vertex prototype (plan item 1)

Measured 2026-10-14 offline, Tools/dc_vertex_proto.py (game op formulas from MUD_VoxelProbe.c v040: Carve, CarveRut
in 6 passes of 0.08 m to cap 0.33 m, CutTrench 4 layers; seed v = top - y; Polygonize + Quad meshing). Grid 12 x 12 m,
CELL 0.5. Measured region x, z 1.5..10.5. "True" = the same op formulas evaluated continuously (0.025 m heightfield,
vertical steps filled with wall points). Distances in m. Control test first: the QEF solver returns an exact corner,
a crease (free axis kept at the mass point) and a flat plane (only y moved) - passed.

Methods: mean = today's CellPos. dc_tri = QEF with normals from the trilinear field inside the cell (plan B1).
dc_cd = QEF with central-difference gradients at the edge's two samples, lerped. dc_exact = QEF with the TRUE
field's normals - impossible in game, the upper bound (reachability check: if it does not help, no normal source can).
QEF: Jacobi 3x3, DC_SVD_EPS 0.1, outside the cell box + 10 % -> mass point ("clamped").
Tiny = area < 1 cm2; sliver = smallest angle < 10 deg (IDA 2026-10-14: the engine weights every triangle's normal
the same and CreateTriMesh keeps degenerate ones). Folded = triangle faces against its edge's air direction.
Cost = Python vertex time vs mean (pure-Python loops, as Enforce would run them).

- **Ruts: no.** The rut's real edge is a ~0.17 m vertical step (CarveRut cuts to half depth at RUT_R, nothing past
  it). Every method draws it as a ~30-40 deg slope, the exact-normal upper bound too (31-42 deg). The step falls
  BETWEEN 0.5 m sample columns, so the samples don't hold it - the vertex can't find what the field never stored.
  See rut_straight.png: all four lines lie on top of each other at both rut edges. Sharper ruts need finer
  samples (plan Step E, 0.25 m), not a better vertex.
- **Craters / trenches: yes, modestly.** dc_cd puts the surface 41 % (big crater) and 48 % (trench) closer on
  average and catches the crater rim and trench lip better (crater_big.png, trench.png).
- Cost 1.4-1.6x in the prototype (plan limit 2x) - ok.
- But DC adds what the IDA check warns about: 0-4 folded triangles and 4-29 slivers per shape (mean: 0 folded,
  0-12 slivers). B2 would need the collider filter (drop folded / tiny before CreateTriMesh), and slivers pull
  engine normals with full weight (shading noise, not measurable offline).
- dc_tri (the plan's normal source) gains almost nothing on ruts and trench and folds 12 tris on rut_arc: if B2
  is done at all, use dc_cd.

## Go / no-go (plan: clearly sharper ruts and crater lips, ~0 folds, cost <= 2x)

- Ruts: NO-GO (not sharper; limit is sample spacing, proven by the upper bound).
- Crater lips / trench walls: sharper, cost ok, but not fold-free -> conditional (only with the collider filter).
- Since ruts were B's main reason, my reading: skip B2 for now, go to Step E (finer rut cells), and keep dc_cd
  as a later crater/trench polish. User decides.
