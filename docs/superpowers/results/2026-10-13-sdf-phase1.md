## Design change vs the spec: ghost-cell clamp instead of GetH padding

Cells outside the patch / on kept terrain next to an on cell are "ghosts": their vertex is clamped onto the
shared face and reads only samples on that face. On a patch border those are the border samples both patches
hold as identical copies, so the seam is exact without reading the neighbour (no MarkDirty widening, no
neighbour m_aShow, no coupling to whether the neighbour exists). Padding would need all three.
